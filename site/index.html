Your portfolio and Kira conversation files live on your computer. This website is an introduction and installation guide. It does not host your wallet workspace.

Research connections

RPC uses your custom connection with public fallback by default. Indexers extend token discovery. Browser wallets and local OWS accounts link their public addresses; signing keys stay outside the research agent.

Model connections

Your message and approved context go through your native CLI to its model service. Choose one wallet or the whole portfolio and enable research tools. General chat remains available with wallet access off. Provider and native CLI policies apply.

No signing or trading through chat. You can allow your agent to inspect records and run holdings or price research. Changing model or context starts a fresh conversation.
