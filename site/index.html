K
YOUR WORKSPACE, YOUR CHOICES

Local records.
Explicit context.

Your portfolio and Kira conversation files live on your computer. This website is an introduction and installation guide. It does not host your wallet workspace.

Research connections

Read-only RPC and indexer requests go to the providers you configure. Kira never asks for a seed phrase or private key.

Model connections

Your message and approved context go through your native CLI to its model service. Choose no wallet context, one wallet, or the whole portfolio. Provider and native CLI policies apply.

No signing, trading or automatic research through chat. Changing model or context starts a fresh conversation.
