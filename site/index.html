THE TOOLS ARE YOURS TO USE

Your model.
Your records.
Your call.

Choose the agent.

Kira is a local CLI with portable instructions and optional MCP. Use a terminal-capable agent on a supported host. Each host supplies its model, conversation and approvals.

Keep the research local.

Wallet records stay on your computer. Your chosen agent host controls which queried facts it sends to its model service and how it stores conversations.

Watch with a public address.

No wallet signature is needed for watching. Kira’s research tools do not sign or trade. Optional new-wallet creation uses a local encrypted OWS vault.

Know what a number means.

Saved observations have timestamps and coverage limits. An unknown price stays unknown. A spot estimate is not an executable sale quote.
