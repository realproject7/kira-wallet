Keep the research local.

Your wallet research stays on your computer, ready for your next session. Your agent’s usual data and conversation settings still apply.

Watch with a public address.

No wallet signature is needed for watching. Kira’s research tools do not sign or trade. Optional new-wallet creation uses a local encrypted OWS vault.

Know what a number means.

Saved observations have timestamps and coverage limits. An unknown price stays unknown. A spot estimate is not an executable sale quote.
